What is a car variant?

A variant is a specific version of a car model. It sets the combination of engine, fuel type, gearbox, trim level and features, so one model can have many prices. Trim names such as Sigma, Delta, Zeta and Alpha are Maruti Suzuki’s way of ranking variants from basic to fully loaded.

Example: 2026 Maruti Baleno (India, ex-showroom)

The facelift launched on 5 Sep 2026 and prices range from ₹5.99 lakh to ₹9.99 lakh. Petrol automatic (AMT) versions cost ₹50,000 more than the petrol manual, and CNG costs ₹92,000 more. It uses a 1.2-litre three-cylinder petrol engine with 83 hp and 112 Nm, and the CNG version makes 70 hp and 102 Nm. Claimed economy is 23.80 kpl (manual), 24.77 kpl (AMT) and 33.61 km/kg (CNG). Rivals include the Tata Altroz (₹6 to 10.75 lakh), Hyundai i20 (₹6 to 10.39 lakh) and Toyota Glanza (₹6.73 to 9.99 lakh). Reports differ by a few thousand rupees on some trims, so confirm with a Nexa dealer. Sources: Autocar India · 91wheels · iamabiker
